The [project page](/project) now lists **twenty projects in six themes**, from a cipher toolkit in C to a campus event board in Flask. Each has a minimum scope and an advanced scope. The project is 15% of your final grade and is built in your own GitHub repository from week 5 to week 15, with commit tracking and a live demo on December 28.

**What to do:** read the catalog, pick your **three ranked preferences**, and email them to fucar@firat.edu.tr by **Monday, October 12, 23:59** (one line per choice, plus a sentence on why the first one). Your own idea is welcome in the same format. Assignments are announced in week 5.
