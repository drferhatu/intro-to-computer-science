## Demo day

- Order is posted under [announcements](/announcements) the week before. Each demo: 5 minutes plus 2–3 questions.
- Demo from your own repository in Codespaces or on your laptop. If something crashes, explain what happened and keep going; handling it calmly counts.
- Grading follows the [project rubric](/project#rules); the process part is read from your commit history and tags.

## The last lecture

1. What you now know, as one diagram: bits → C → algorithms and memory → data structures → Python → AI and data → web → people.
2. What the rest of the degree adds, and which course goes deeper into which week.
3. How to keep going: CS50's follow-on courses, open source, your own projects, and the loop (watch, build, push).
