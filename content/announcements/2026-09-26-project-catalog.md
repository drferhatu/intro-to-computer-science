The [project page](/project) explains the semester project: 15% of your final grade, built in your own GitHub repository from week 5 to week 15, with commit tracking and a live demo on December 28.

Each student receives a **personal project** in week 5 (October 19), assigned by the instructor from a bank of twenty projects across six themes, from text and ciphers in C to a small web app. Every project has a minimum scope and an advanced scope, sized for one person. Until then: do the labs; they are the practice ground for the project.
